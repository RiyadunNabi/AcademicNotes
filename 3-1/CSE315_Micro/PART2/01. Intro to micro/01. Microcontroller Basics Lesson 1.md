# 5. Efficient for High-Level Languages

Much easier.

Modern microcontrollers are designed to run C programs efficiently.

Since you already know C/C++, you'll mostly program microcontrollers in C.

# 1. 8051 Family

One of the oldest and most famous microcontrollers.

```text
Intel

↓

8051
```

Characteristics

- Very popular in education
- Simple architecture
- 8-bit
- Older technology

Many universities still teach it because it's easy to learn.

---

# 2. AVR Family

This is the family you'll most likely use in your lab.

Examples

```text
ATmega8

ATmega16

ATmega32

ATmega328P
```

These are **all AVR microcontrollers**.

Characteristics

- Fast
- Easy to program in C
- Low power
- Used in many embedded projects

One famous board:

```text
Arduino Uno

↓

ATmega328P
```

So when people program Arduino, they're actually programming an **AVR microcontroller**.

---

# 3. PIC Family

Made by **Microchip Technology**.

Characteristics

- Very common in industry
- Reliable
- Large variety of models
- Used in commercial products

---
