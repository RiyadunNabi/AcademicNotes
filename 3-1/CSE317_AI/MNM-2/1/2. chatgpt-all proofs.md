# Viva Questions

### Q1. Why do we use Union Bound?

**Answer:**

The previous proof only bounded the probability that **one bad hypothesis** fools ERM. Since there may be many bad hypotheses in the hypothesis class, Union Bound is used to bound the probability that **any** bad hypothesis fools ERM.

---

### Q2. What is the Union Bound?

**Answer:**

For events $A_1,A_2,\ldots,A_n$,

$$
P\left(\bigcup_i A_i\right)
\le
\sum_i P(A_i).
$$

It states that the probability of **at least one** event occurring is at most the sum of their individual probabilities.

---

### Q3. Why must the hypothesis class be finite?

**Answer:**

Because the bound contains the factor $|H|$. If $|H|$ is infinite, the bound is no longer useful, so the theorem requires a finite hypothesis class.

}
$$

This is the theorem professors most commonly ask students to derive.
